Community

The conversation happens on LinkedIn. The record lives here.

Accountable mail practitioners are scattered across mailrooms, in-plants, service bureaus, and agency print shops, and most of them already spend their professional time on LinkedIn. So that is where we discuss, and this is where we keep what the discussion produced.

Follow the page

We post when something changes: a price adjustment, a specification revision, an MTAC readout, or a new page in the knowledge center. No reposted industry filler, and no promotion of anybody's product, including our members'.

Comments are where the useful material actually comes from. When a question in a comment thread turns out to affect more than one operation, it becomes a page here, and the person who raised it gets credit unless they would rather not.

Follow on LinkedIn Browse the knowledge center

How a comment becomes a page

Someone raises a problem in a comment thread. We check whether it is specific to one operation or general. If it is general, we research it against USPS source documentation, publish it here with citations, and link back into the thread. That loop is the entire content strategy.

What we do not do

We do not sell lists, we do not pass member contact information to suppliers, and we do not let anyone buy placement in the knowledge center. If a page recommends an approach, it is because the USPS documentation supports it.

What people are asking

July 28, 2026

"Does automating Certificate of Mailing get rid of the BMEU trip?"

No, and this is the most common misconception about the service. Automation removes the paper handling around presentment, not the presentment itself. Full answer.

July 21, 2026

"We are being denied tracking data for our own mail. Why?"

Almost always an account structure problem, and since April 2026 it fails harder than it used to. Full answer.

July 14, 2026

"Our policy says return receipt requested. Does that mean the green card?"

Usually not. It generally describes the service rather than the physical form, and the gap between the two prices widened in July. Full answer.

Get set up

Two minutes, and you are on the list

Enrolling is not the same as joining the association. Enrollment is free, takes an email address, and gets you the update notice whenever something changes in accountable mail. Membership is a separate, paid decision you can make later.

What enrolled subscribers get

  • Change notices. Price adjustments, specification revisions, and deadline reminders, sent when they happen rather than monthly.
  • New pages. A short note when the knowledge center gains something in your area of interest.
  • Public MTAC summaries. The non-member version of what came out of each quarterly meeting.

What members get on top of that

  • Full written MTAC readouts and the issue tracker
  • Advance notice ahead of public announcements where we have it
  • A vote on the positions the association carries to USPS
  • Work group and user group participation

Contribute

Three ways to put something in

Easiest

Ask a question

Comment on a LinkedIn post or email us. Questions from real operations set the publishing order for the knowledge center.

Most valuable

Report a variance

Acceptance practice differs between facilities. If yours does something other facilities do not, tell us. Documented variance is what moves an issue at MTAC.

Most work

Write a page

If you know a corner of this subject well, draft it. We edit for house style, verify every claim against USPS documentation, and credit you.
