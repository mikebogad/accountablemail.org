# Form names and electronic status

The single source of truth for form terminology on this site. USPS language leads. Where the
industry uses a different name, we give the official title first and the common name second.

Verified July 2026 against USPS Postal Bulletin, Postal Explorer, PostalPro, and the Federal
Register. Re-verify on any DMM revision affecting 503 or 608.

## Rules that must not be broken

### 1. PS Form 3817 and PS Form 3800 are paper only

Neither has an electronic version. Never write "electronic 3817" or "electronic 3800." PS Form 3817
is a retail counter transaction. PS Form 3800 is the paper Certified Mail receipt, and commercial
mailers submitting electronically do not use it at all; they apply their own barcoded labels
carrying the Certified Mail service indicator.

### 2. Electronic Return Receipt is not an electronic PS Form 3811

PS Form 3811 is a paper card. Electronic Return Receipt is a different fulfillment of the same
underlying Return Receipt service. This distinction matters when a policy document specifies the
form by number, because specifying "PS Form 3811" specifies paper.

### 3. Automated COM uses Postal Wizard and Mail.dat only

USPS lists three general eDoc channels: Postal Wizard, Mail.dat, and Mail.XML. **Automated
Certificate of Mailing uses only the first two.** USPS documentation states PS Form 3877 is
available for upload via Postal Wizard or Mail.dat, and the rulemaking establishing the automated
option covers PS Form 3606-D, PS Form 3665, and PS Form 3877 uploaded to PostalOne! with payment
through EPS. Mail.XML is a general eDoc channel and is not a COM path. Do not carry the generic
three-channel description onto a COM page.

### 4. "Firm book" is ambiguous. Disambiguate every time

Both PS Form 3877 and PS Form 3665 get called the firm book in conversation. They are different
forms serving different services:

- **PS Form 3877** lists article numbers for accountable services such as Certified Mail, COD,
  Priority Mail Express, and insured mail. USPS states it is **not technically a Certificate of
  Mailing form**.
- **PS Form 3665** lists pieces for Certificate of Mailing.

For listing Certificate of Mailing, USPS directs mailers to PS Form 3665 or PS Form 3606-D.

### 5. Only three forms are automated

PS Form 3606-D, PS Form 3665, and PS Form 3877. Nothing else in this family has an automated path.
