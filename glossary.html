Reference

Accountable mail glossary

Every form number, file format, system, and acronym you will hear in a Business Mail Entry Unit or an MTAC meeting, defined once and in plain language. Sorted alphabetically.

Reviewed July 2026 38 terms Additions welcome
Accountable mail terms
TermTypeDefinition
Accountable mailTermMail sent with an extra service that produces a record, such as Certified Mail, Certificate of Mailing, insured mail, COD, or Priority Mail Express.
BCGSystemBusiness Customer Gateway. The USPS portal through which business mailers access most electronic services, including Postal Wizard.
BME / BMEUFacilityBusiness Mail Entry, and the Business Mail Entry Unit. The acceptance point where commercial mail is presented and verified, and where postage statements are finalized.
Certificate of Bulk MailingServiceA certificate stating a count of identical weight pieces. Filed on PS Form 3606-D domestically, PS Form 3606 internationally. Does not name addressees and provides no delivery record.
Certificate of MailingServiceEvidence that mailpieces were presented to USPS for mailing. Presented only at the time of mailing. No delivery record, and USPS retains no copies.
Certified MailServiceAn extra service adding an article number, a mailing receipt, and a USPS retained delivery record to First-Class Mail or Priority Mail.
COMAbbreviationCertificate of Mailing.
CRIDIdentifierCustomer Registration ID. The USPS identifier for a business location. Mailer IDs are parented under a CRID, which is why CRID structure controls data access.
DMMPublicationDomestic Mail Manual. The USPS regulations. Extra services, including Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing, live in section 503.
eDocTermElectronic documentation. Mailing data submitted to PostalOne! before the mail is presented, replacing hardcopy reports.
Electronic Return ReceiptServiceReturn Receipt delivered electronically rather than as a physical green card. Lower fee and faster turnaround than PS Form 3811.
EPSSystemEnterprise Payment System. The USPS account used to pay postage and fees electronically. Required for electronic PS Form 3665 submission.
Extra serviceTermA service purchased in addition to postage, such as Certified Mail, Return Receipt, Restricted Delivery, or Certificate of Mailing.
Firm bookTermAmbiguous in conversation. It can mean PS Form 3877, Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail, or PS Form 3665, Certificate of Mailing — Firm. Different forms, different services. Ask which one is meant.
Firm sheetTermAn individual sheet within a firm book. USPS uses the term parenthetically for both PS Form 3665 and PS Form 3877.
Full-ServiceProgramThe USPS program requiring electronic documentation and unique barcodes, in exchange for pricing and data benefits.
IMbBarcodeIntelligent Mail barcode. The barcode carrying routing and identification data on letter and flat mail.
IV-MTRSystemInformed Visibility Mail Tracking and Reporting. The USPS platform providing mailers with scan and visibility data.
Mail.datFormatA mailing database format containing all information about a mailing except addresses. USPS describes it as the established industry standard for eDoc submission.
Mail.XMLFormatA messaging format for sending required mailing information directly to USPS, available in advanced software packages. One of three general eDoc channels, but not a submission path for automated Certificate of Mailing, which uses Postal Wizard and Mail.dat only.
MIDIdentifierMailer ID. A USPS assigned identifier embedded in barcodes that attributes mail to a specific mailer. Parented under a CRID.
MTACBodyMailers' Technical Advisory Committee. The forum where USPS shares technical information with the mailing industry and receives recommendations. Associations hold the membership, not companies.
Notice 123PublicationThe USPS price list. Where current postage and extra service fees are published.
PostalOne!SystemThe USPS system that receives electronic documentation from business mailers and manages postage statements.
Postal WizardSystemManual entry of postage statement information into the PostalOne! website. Available for Full-Service mailings under 10,000 pieces, and limited to simple mailings.
PS Form 3606FormCertificate of Bulk Mailing — International. The international counterpart to PS Form 3606-D.
PS Form 3606-DFormCertificate of Bulk Mailing — Domestic. Count only. Cannot be used as a certificate for individual pieces or itemized lists. Uploadable before presentment.
PS Form 3665FormCertificate of Mailing — Firm. Known in the industry as the firm book. For three or more pieces. Submittable electronically to PostalOne! via Postal Wizard or Mail.dat, with postage paid from an EPS account.
PS Form 3800FormCertified Mail Receipt. The paper receipt completed at a retail counter, carrying the article number. Paper only, with no electronic version. Commercial mailers submitting electronically do not use it.
PS Form 3811FormDomestic Return Receipt, commonly called the green card. Physically returns the recipient's signature to the sender. Paper only. Electronic Return Receipt is a separate fulfillment of the service, not an electronic 3811.
PS Form 3817FormCertificate of Mailing. For fewer than three individual pieces presented at retail. Paper only, with no electronic version.
PS Form 3877FormFirm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail, known in the industry as the Accountable Mail firm book. Lists article numbers for accountable services. Not a Certificate of Mailing form. Uploadable via Postal Wizard or Mail.dat only.
RDSTermRound date stamp. The postmark applied at acceptance, which establishes the mailing date on a Certificate of Mailing.
Restricted DeliveryServiceAn extra service limiting who may accept a mailpiece, used when a rule requires delivery to a named individual.
Return ReceiptServiceAn extra service capturing the recipient's signature, available as a physical green card or electronically.
SSFFormatShipping Services File. The electronic file format carrying package and accountable mail data to USPS.
TEMProgramTesting Environment for Mailers. The USPS certification environment that Mail.dat and Mail.XML submitters must pass through before production.
UAATermUndeliverable as addressed. A delivery outcome caused by an address problem, which generally weakens a notice claim.
