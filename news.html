July 12, 2026
Pricing

New prices take effect: Certified Mail rises to $5.55

The July 12 mailing services adjustment raised the Certified Mail fee from $5.30 to $5.55, electronic Return Receipt from $2.82 to $2.91, and the PS Form 3811 green card from $4.40 to $4.65. Note that the gap between the two Return Receipt formats widened, since the card rose 25 cents while the electronic version rose 9 cents. The differential is now $1.74 per piece.

April 1, 2026
Systems

USPS tracking data access control takes effect

New access control requirements now apply to Tracking APIs, webhook subscriptions, and scan event extracts. Access depends on authorization to the Mailer ID in the barcode rather than on possession of a tracking number. Direct shippers using their own registered Mailer ID keep no-cost access. Organizations with fragmented account structures may find they cannot retrieve data for their own mail.

January 18, 2026
Regulation

DMM 608.11 defines postmarks, and says they do not prove your mailing date

USPS incorporated new section 608.11, "Postmarks and Postal Possession," into the January 18 edition of the online DMM. The standards became effective December 24, 2025. The section states that a postmark date does not necessarily indicate the first day USPS had possession, and it names Certificate of Mailing, Certified Mail, and Registered Mail as the services that prove the date of acceptance. No operational change, significant evidentiary consequences.

December 15, 2025
Data

FY2025 RPW: Certified Mail grows again while the mailstream shrinks

USPS reported Certified Mail volume of 159.8 million pieces in fiscal 2025, up 2.1 percent, against a 3.2 percent decline in total market dominant mail and a 5.1 percent decline in First-Class Mail. Second consecutive year of growth. Revenue rose 12.6 percent to $798.3 million, so most of the revenue increase reflects fee increases rather than demand.
