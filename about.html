About the association

An industry voice for organizations that mail with proof

The Accountable Mail Association exists for one reason: Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing are essential to a very large number of organizations, and nobody has been representing them specifically. We publish what we learn, and we carry member issues into the rooms where the rules get written.

Mission

The Accountable Mail Association advances the modernization, integrity, and accessibility of Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing, so that any organization required to prove it mailed something can do so accurately, affordably, and at scale. Adopted by the founding board, 2026

Independent and vendor neutral

The association does not endorse, certify, or recommend commercial products. Suppliers are welcome as associate members and contribute real technical expertise, but they do not control the agenda, the board majority, or any position we take before the Postal Service. The bylaws state this plainly, and the knowledge center follows the same rule.

Why a dedicated association

Accountable mail is the quiet exception in a shrinking industry. While most mail classes decline, organizations keep sending Certified Mail and Certificates of Mailing because a statute, a contract, a regulator, or a judge requires it. That demand is not discretionary, and it is not going away.

The services themselves still carry decades of manual process: green cards, firm mailing books, round stamped receipts, and hand filed proof. Electronic alternatives exist, but adoption is uneven, acceptance practice varies by facility, and the mailers who depend on these services most rarely sit at the table where the rules get written.

Existing associations serve presort, nonprofit, catalog, parcel, periodical, and in-plant interests very well, and we intend to work alongside them rather than duplicate them. None of them exists specifically to represent accountable mail. We do.

What we do

  • Publish. The knowledge center is free and open to everyone, member or not.
  • Represent. We carry member issues into MTAC and into direct engagement with USPS product management.
  • Warn early. Members get advance notice of pricing, specification, and system changes affecting accountable mail.
  • Convene. We bring mailers, agencies, and service providers together around problems that none of them can fix alone.

MTAC representation

A seat at the table, on your behalf

The Mailers' Technical Advisory Committee is where the Postal Service shares technical information with the mailing industry and receives advice and recommendations in return. Associations hold the membership, not individual companies, and each association appoints an executive and representatives who carry member issues into the room.

That structure is exactly why a dedicated accountable mail association matters. Certified Mail and Certificate of Mailing issues currently surface inside broader agendas, competing with presort economics, parcel growth, and marketing mail response rates. We intend to bring them forward on their own terms, with member data behind them.

Quarterly MTAC meetings take place at Postal Service headquarters at 475 L'Enfant Plaza SW in Washington, DC. Members receive a written readout after every meeting, plus participation opportunities in the user groups and work groups where the detailed work actually happens.

Membership

Four categories, one agenda

Membership is open to any organization with a stake in accountable mail. Voting weight sits with the organizations that send the mail, by design. The knowledge center stays free regardless.

Membership categories and dues
Category Who it is for Rights Annual dues
Mailer Enterprises and in-plant operations that send accountable mail, including financial services, insurance, mortgage servicing, healthcare, utilities, and legal. Full voting rights, work group eligibility, MTAC readouts. To be set by the founding board
Government Federal, state, and local agencies, courts, revenue departments, and public sector print and mail operations. Full voting rights, work group eligibility, MTAC readouts. Reduced rate
Service provider Print and mail service providers, presort bureaus, and outsourced mail operations producing accountable mail for clients. Full voting rights, work group eligibility, MTAC readouts. To be set by the founding board
Associate Software, equipment, and consulting suppliers serving the accountable mail market. Non-voting on supplier-specific matters, full participation in technical work. To be set by the founding board
